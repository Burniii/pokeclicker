Every species has 5 research tasks. Each finished task gives a ★ (+1% attack for that Pokémon). Your ★ raise your Research Rank, which unlocks rewards, expedition and ranch slots.

Pokémon with the fewest ★ first. Hover for the tasks.

Expeditions unlock after you defeat the Champion of Kanto.

Send up to 3 Pokémon on a mission. They don't battle while away. Matching types (+25%) and strong Pokémon raise the success chance. Rewards scale with the duration; a failed mission still gives bond.

New expedition

The ranch opens once you reach Johto.

Ranch residents don't battle but produce resources by type every hour (up to hours stored) and grow their bond. More hearts mean more production.

Move a Pokémon to the ranch

Every Pokémon has a nature with a small bonus. Mints change the nature. From Paldea on, Tera Shards give a Pokémon a Tera type that is used in battle when it is more effective.
