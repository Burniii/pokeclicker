# Map resources

- `magiscarf-tileset-v3.png` — "Tileset ver.3 [Free]" by **Magiscarf**
  (https://www.deviantart.com/magiscarf/art/Tileset-ver-3-Free-690477146), published as free to use.
  Credit: Magiscarf. The sheet was saved from Pinterest with a baked-in checkerboard background;
  `magiscarf_assets.py` removes it and cuts the sheet into objects.
- Further sheets saved from the pins in `references.json` (use cleared by the project owner, sources in that file):
  `chaoticcherrycake-public-tiles.png` (ChaoticCherryCake, "Pokemon Tileset From Public Tiles"),
  `zerudez-public-tileset.png` (zerudez, "Public Tileset"), `zeo254-resurgent.png` (Zeo254, "Tileset Pokemon Resurgent"),
  `saurav-buildings.png` / `saurav-nature.png` (Saurav, PokéCommunity), `erma96-buildings.jpg` (Erma96, "give credits"),
  `frlg-buildings-1.png` / `frlg-buildings-2.png` (FireRed/LeafGreen rips, Spriters Resource), `bacon-objects.jpg` (Bacon),
  `houses-mixed.jpg`, `phyromatical-mountains.png` (Phyromatical), `nature-black.jpg`. `sheet_assets.py` cuts them into objects.
  Excluded on the author's request: Red-eX's "Pokemon Dawn Tileset" ("Please do not use these tiles").
- `references.json` — catalogue of 620 Pinterest pins (Pokémon fangame maps, town maps, tilesets,
  island maps, festival scenes) used only as style references. Nothing from these pins is copied into
  the game unless it is listed above with its licence.
- Ground textures, buildings, palms and boats come from the Alola maps already in the game
  (`src/assets/images/alola-*.png`, see `alola_assets.py`).
